Roasting at Sea Level: The Goan Micro-Climate

Roasting coffee at sea level in Goa's humid, tropical climate presents a unique set of challenges and opportunities. The high ambient humidity slows down the initial moisture loss of the green beans, allowing for a longer, more even development phase inside our drum roaster. This results in an exceptionally smooth, low-acid cup with enhanced body and sweetness.

Our state-of-the-art roastery in Panjim is climate-controlled to ensure consistency, but we embrace the natural coastal influence that gives our beans their signature tropical character. Every batch is meticulously monitored to capture the delicate balance of fruitiness and deep caramelization.

Interactive Brew Guides

Unlock the full potential of our beans with our step-by-step brewing instructions.

V60 Pour Over Guide

The V60 highlights the bright, floral, and complex tasting notes of our single-origin coffees. It produces a clean, light-bodied cup with zero sediment.

1

Grind 15g of coffee to a medium-fine consistency (resembling table salt).

2

Rinse the paper filter with hot water to remove any paper taste and preheat the dripper.

3

Add coffee, pour 40g of water at 93°C, and let it bloom for 30 seconds to release trapped gases.

4

Pour the remaining water in circular motions up to 250g. Total brew time should be around 3 minutes.

South Indian Filter Guide

A traditional method that produces a thick, strong decoction, perfect for mixing with hot, frothed milk and sugar.

1

Add 20g of finely ground coffee (with 15-20% chicory) to the upper chamber of the brass filter.

2

Gently press the coffee down with the plunger, leaving it inside the chamber.

3

Pour boiling water over the plunger, fill the chamber, close the lid, and let it drip for 15 minutes.

4

Mix the rich decoction with hot frothed milk and sugar, pouring it between cups to create a frothy head.

French Press Guide

An immersion brewing method that extracts the full body and rich oils of the coffee, resulting in a heavy, textured mouthfeel.

1

Grind 30g of coffee to a very coarse consistency (resembling sea salt).

2

Add coffee to the press, pour 500ml of hot water (93°C), and stir gently to saturate all grounds.

3

Place the lid on top without plunging, and let the coffee steep undisturbed for 4 minutes.

4

Slowly press the plunger down, pour immediately to prevent over-extraction, and enjoy.

Our Commitment to Sustainability

At Goa Roast Co., sustainability is at the core of everything we do. We believe that coffee production should be environmentally responsible and socially equitable. That's why we partner directly with farmers in the Western Ghats who use sustainable farming practices, such as shade-grown coffee and organic fertilizers. We are committed to reducing our environmental impact, from our roasting process to our packaging. We use energy-efficient roasters and are constantly looking for ways to minimize waste. We also believe in fair wages for our farmers and are committed to supporting the communities where our coffee is grown. We believe that when we support the people and the land, we can create a more sustainable future for everyone. Thank you for supporting our commitment to sustainability and for choosing coffee that makes a positive impact.
